# Hymn audio attribution

12 short excerpts (~42s, 64 kbps mono) bundled for the in-app hymn
player. All files were sourced from Wikimedia Commons and the Internet Archive and are either Public
Domain or licensed CC BY-SA. Each excerpt was trimmed, fade-in/out applied and
loudness-normalised; no other changes were made.

CC BY-SA tracks require attribution and share-alike on redistribution — keep
this file with the assets and surface the credits in the app's about screen.
