IMPORTANT - please note the distinction between the two halves of this
recording. The first half is demonstration-mode fixture data; the second half
is the live instance. They differ by roughly an order of magnitude. Section 3.5
of the final project report explains why this matters: the "3-6 second
pipeline" figure quoted in the Phase 3 document and in the earlier presentation
deck traces to those fixtures and was never a measurement. The measured figure
is 27.5 seconds, and the report records the requirement (NFR1, five seconds) as
NOT met.
